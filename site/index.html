Healthcare data, handled with care.

A nightly data pipeline that takes messy, realistic healthcare records, cleans and checks them, protects privacy, and turns them into trustworthy reports. Every step, test and decision is public. Nothing here is real patient data.

How the data flows

Four layers, each with a clear job and a way to prove it.

Bronze

Raw files exactly as they arrive, kept unchanged.

Silver

Checked and cleaned. Bad records are set aside with the reason, never silently dropped. Identifiers masked.

Gold

Trusted business measures: denial rates, length of stay, lab flags.

Platinum

Privacy-protected tables, dashboards and questions answered in plain English.

Work and quality, drawn from the project's own records.

This is a demonstration project built on synthetic data. It is not medical advice and contains no real patient information. Controls are aligned with and mapped to HIPAA safeguards; no certification or compliance is claimed. Source code and history: github.com/aimozart/hippagrchealthcare.
